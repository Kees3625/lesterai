# General IT Troubleshooting — Common Fixes

Quick self-help steps for the most common computer issues. If a step says to
contact IT, submit a ticket at **https://support.capitalvacations.com**
(IT Help Desk: 8am–10pm EST, 7 days a week).

## Start here: restart first

The single most effective fix. Before anything else, **save your work and restart
the computer** (Start → Power → Restart, not just Shut Down). This clears most
temporary glitches with apps, audio, displays, and connectivity. A surprising number
of issues are solved by a restart.

## Computer won't turn on

1. Confirm the power cable is firmly connected at both ends; if it's a laptop, make
   sure it's charging (check for a charging light).
2. Try a different outlet, and a different charger/cable if you have one.
3. Hold the power button down for **10 seconds** to force it off, then press it again
   to turn it on.
4. For laptops: remove any docking station and try powering on with just the charger.
5. Still nothing? Submit a ticket and note any lights, sounds, or error messages.

## Computer is running slow

1. Restart the computer (see above) — this alone fixes most slowness.
2. Close programs and browser tabs you aren't using.
3. Check that updates aren't installing in the background (Start → Settings →
   Windows Update).
4. If it's been weeks since a restart, that's often the cause — make restarting a
   daily habit.
5. Persistent slowness after a restart → submit a ticket.

## Second monitor not detected

1. Check the video cable (HDMI/DisplayPort/USB-C) is firmly seated at both the monitor
   and the computer/dock; unplug and replug it.
2. Make sure the monitor is powered on and set to the correct input source.
3. Press **Windows key + P** and choose **Extend**.
4. Reseat the dock connection if you use a docking station, or restart with the dock
   connected.
5. Still not detected → submit a ticket with the monitor and dock model if known.

## A program is frozen or won't respond

1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Esc** to open Task Manager.
2. Select the frozen program, then click **End task**.
3. Reopen the program.
4. If it keeps freezing, restart the computer; if it still happens, submit a ticket
   with the program name and what you were doing.

## No sound, or microphone/camera not working (Teams calls)

1. Check the volume isn't muted (speaker icon, bottom-right) and the volume is up.
2. In Teams: **... → Settings → Devices** and confirm the correct speaker, microphone,
   and camera are selected.
3. Make sure no other app is using the camera/mic, then restart Teams.
4. Plug headsets/webcams directly into the computer (not through an unpowered hub) and
   reconnect.
5. Restart the computer if the device still isn't detected. Still broken → submit a ticket.

## Printer won't print

1. Check the printer is on, has paper, and has no error lights or paper jams.
2. Make sure you selected the right printer in the print dialog.
3. Open the print queue (Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → your
   printer → Open print queue) and cancel any stuck jobs.
4. Turn the printer off, wait 15 seconds, and turn it back on.
5. Restart your computer and try again. Still failing → submit a ticket with the
   printer name/location and any error shown.

## Internet, WiFi, or VPN problems

Quick checks when the internet isn't working:
1. Check whether other sites or devices are also affected.
2. **If your computer is connected to a docking station, unplug the docking station from
   power for about 7 seconds, then plug it back in.** This resets the dock's network
   connection and often restores the internet.
3. Turn WiFi off and on, or restart your computer.

If these steps don't fix it, submit a ticket at https://support.capitalvacations.com.

## Email (Outlook / O365) not sending or receiving

1. Confirm you're connected to the internet (open a website).
2. In Outlook, check the bottom status bar — if it says "Working Offline," click
   **Send/Receive → Work Offline** to toggle it back online.
3. Restart Outlook, then restart the computer.
4. Locked out of email or can't sign in? Submit a ticket at https://support.capitalvacations.com.
5. Still stuck → submit a ticket.

## Website or browser acting up

Clearing your browser history/cache fixes many website problems — you can ask me how to
clear your browser history. If a specific company site is down for everyone, submit a
ticket at https://support.capitalvacations.com.

## Keyboard or mouse not working

1. For wired: unplug and plug into a different USB port.
2. For wireless: check/replace the batteries and confirm the USB receiver is plugged in,
   or that Bluetooth is on and the device is paired.
3. Try the device on another computer if possible to isolate the issue.
4. Restart the computer. Still dead → submit a ticket.

## When in doubt

Submit a ticket at **https://support.capitalvacations.com** with: what device you're on,
what you were trying to do, the exact error message, and what you've already tried.
That gets you helped faster.
