# Password Reset & Account Lockout

**Covers:** forgotten passwords, locked-out accounts, and login
failures for Windows, email/O365, SPI, RDG, and TSW.
*(Based on 217 "Password Issues" tickets — the most common are lockouts and resets.)*

## Reset your Windows / email (O365) password

1. Go to **https://passwordreset.microsoftonline.com** and follow the prompts to reset
   it yourself.
2. After resetting, sign out and back in everywhere (Outlook, Teams, phone mail app).

## I'm locked out (not just a forgotten password)

A locked-out account has to be unlocked by IT — there's no self-service for this.
**Submit a ticket at https://support.capitalvacations.com** and include your username
and which system you're locked out of.

## SPI / RDG / TSW login problems

These run in a remote desktop session and are separate from your Windows password.
If you can't get in or the session seems stuck:

1. Inside the remote session, press **Ctrl + Alt + End** to bring up the security screen.
2. Choose **Sign out**, then log back in.
3. If that doesn't fix it, **submit a ticket to IT** with the system name (SPI, RDG, or TSW)
   and the exact error you're seeing.

## Still stuck?

Submit a ticket at **https://support.capitalvacations.com**. The IT Help Desk is
available **8am–10pm EST, 7 days a week**.

When you submit a ticket, include: your username, which system you can't get into, and
the exact error message.
