# Common Windows & Computer Fixes

Quick self-service steps for everyday Windows issues. If a fix here doesn't resolve your problem, submit a ticket at support.capitalvacations.com.

## Change screen brightness
- **Laptop:** Click the battery/network icons at the bottom-right to open Quick Settings, then drag the **brightness slider**. Or go to **Settings > System > Display** and adjust **Brightness**. Many laptops also have brightness keys on the top row (look for sun icons), sometimes used with the **Fn** key.
- **Desktop with an external monitor:** Windows can't control brightness on most external monitors — use the **physical buttons on the monitor itself** (usually on the front edge or underside).

## No sound / adjust volume
- Click the **speaker icon** (bottom-right) and check the volume slider isn't muted or at zero.
- Click the **arrow/device name** above the slider and make sure the correct **output device** is selected (e.g., your headset vs. monitor speakers).
- Check the volume/mute keys on your keyboard, and that headphones are fully plugged in.

## Pick the right sound device (Sound settings)
Use this when sound is coming out of the wrong place (e.g., the monitor instead of your headset), or your mic/headset isn't being heard.

- Right-click the **speaker icon** (bottom-right) and choose **Sound settings** (or go to **Settings > System > Sound**).
- Under **Output**, select the device you actually want to hear from (e.g., your **headset** or **speakers**) — you'll hear audio switch over.
- Under **Input**, select the **microphone** you want to use (e.g., your headset mic), and speak to watch the **volume bar** move to confirm it's picking you up.
- If a device isn't listed, make sure it's plugged in / powered on, then click **Refresh** or unplug and replug it.
- For calls specifically, also set the device inside the app (e.g., **Teams > Settings > Devices**), since apps can use a different device than Windows.

## Reconnect to Wi-Fi
- Click the **network icon** (bottom-right), pick the network, and select **Connect**.
- Staff devices use **CV Private**; if you can't connect to it, submit a ticket (the password isn't shared out).
- Still no internet? Restart the computer and try again.

## Second monitor not showing
- Press **Windows key + P** and choose **Extend** (or Duplicate).
- If the screen still isn't detected, go to **Settings > System > Display > Detect**.
- Check that the monitor's cable is firmly seated at both ends and the monitor is powered on.

## A program is frozen
- Press **Ctrl + Shift + Esc** to open **Task Manager**.
- Select the unresponsive program and click **End task**.
- Reopen the program. If it keeps freezing, restart your computer.

## Computer running slow
- **Restart** the computer — this clears most slowdowns (don't just close the lid; do a full restart).
- Close programs and browser tabs you aren't using.
- Make sure Windows isn't mid-update (**Settings > Windows Update**).

## Take a screenshot
- Press **Windows key + Shift + S** to select an area to capture, then paste it (**Ctrl + V**) into an email, Teams, or a document.

## Bluetooth / headset pairing
- Go to **Settings > Bluetooth & devices**, make sure Bluetooth is **On**, then **Add device**.
- Put the headset/device in pairing mode (check its manual), then select it from the list.

## Camera or mic not working in Teams
- Close and reopen Teams.
- In Teams, go to **Settings > Devices** and confirm the correct **camera, microphone, and speaker** are selected.
- Make sure no other app is using the camera, then restart the computer if needed.

## Keyboard typing wrong characters
- Check the **language indicator** near the clock (e.g., "ENG") — press **Windows key + Spacebar** to switch back to your normal layout.
- Check that **Num Lock** and **Caps Lock** aren't on unintentionally.

## Lock, sign out, or restart
- Press **Ctrl + Alt + Delete** for options to **Lock**, **Sign out**, **Change a password**, or open Task Manager.
- Lock quickly anytime with **Windows key + L**.
