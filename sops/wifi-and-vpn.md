# WiFi & VPN Connectivity

**Covers:** internet not working, connecting to WiFi, WiFi outages at a property/unit, and VPN problems.
*(Based on 476 "WiFi and Network Issues" tickets — about a quarter are VPN-related,
and many report WiFi "OOS" (out of service) at a specific unit/room number.)*

## Internet not working — quick fixes

1. Check whether other sites or devices are also affected.
2. **If your computer is connected to a docking station, unplug the docking station from
   power for about 7 seconds, then plug it back in.** This resets the dock's network
   connection and often restores the internet.
3. Turn WiFi off and on, or restart your computer.
4. Still down? Submit a ticket at **https://support.capitalvacations.com**.

## Connecting to WiFi

There are two WiFi networks (SSIDs):

- **CV** — this is the **guest** network. Password: **CV#12019**. This is the one to give
  to visitors and guests.
- **CV Private** — this is the **staff** network. Its password is **not given out**. If your
  device isn't already connected to CV Private, **submit a ticket at
  https://support.capitalvacations.com** and IT will connect it for you.

If an already-connected device won't reconnect: turn WiFi off and on, or restart the device.

## WiFi is down at a property / unit ("OOS")

Many tickets report WiFi out at a specific unit (e.g. "1820 wifi oos"). To report one:
1. Note the **exact unit/room/building number** — IT needs it to locate the access point.
2. **Submit a ticket at https://support.capitalvacations.com** with the unit number and
   how many units are affected.

## VPN — setting up and connecting (OpenVPN2)

Get everything from the company VPN portal — **https://openvpn2.capitalvacations.com**.
Do **not** download OpenVPN from openvpn.net or any other website.

1. **Go to the VPN portal:** open a browser and go to **https://openvpn2.capitalvacations.com**,
   choose **"Sign in with SAML"**, and log in with your company email credentials.
   *(If you get a security warning, choose Advanced and proceed.)*
2. **Download your connection profile:** once signed in, select **"Connection Profile"**
   and download the configuration file to your computer.
3. **Import it:** open the downloaded profile — the OpenVPN client should launch and import it.
   If the OpenVPN client isn't installed on your computer, **submit a ticket at
   https://support.capitalvacations.com** to have IT install it — don't download it
   yourself from openvpn.net.
4. **Connect:** open the OpenVPN client and select **Connect**.
5. **Confirm:** a browser window may open confirming successful sign-in — once it does,
   you can close it.

### VPN troubleshooting
- Make sure you have working internet first (open any website).
- If connecting fails, fully quit and reopen the OpenVPN client, then try again.
- If it still fails, submit a ticket at https://support.capitalvacations.com with the step it failed on.

## Still stuck?

Submit a ticket at **https://support.capitalvacations.com**. The IT Help Desk is
available **8am–10pm EST, 7 days a week**. Include your location/unit number
(for WiFi) or what step the VPN failed on.
